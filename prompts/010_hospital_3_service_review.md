# Hospital 3 service review

Resolve only recurring Hospital 3 descriptions that remain tied after safe
text and unit-basis matching.

- Treat the merged Base Agreement, Appendix B, and Amendment No. 1 as the
  authority.
- Never let a billed rate override a clear textual service match.
- For a genuine textual tie, inspect all occurrences together. Accept a
  mapping only when unit basis and every observed rate consistently correspond
  to one candidate's documented base, amended, bundled, or uplifted rate.
- Record the line count, unit basis, observed rates, selected clause, and a
  concise reason.
- Leave one-off descriptions with contradictory clinical or service tokens as
  `unknown_service`; do not invent a mapping.
